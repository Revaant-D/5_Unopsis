# Plans and Pricing

Unopsis offers three plans. The Free plan costs nothing, allows three connectors, and processes
up to two hundred notifications per day. The Pro plan costs eight dollars per month, allows
fifteen connectors, and processes up to five thousand notifications per day. The Team plan
costs twenty dollars per user per month, allows unlimited connectors, and processes up to fifty
thousand notifications per day across the whole team. All three plans include the full
filtering engine, the unified inbox, mobile push, and the web application. The paid plans are
distinguished by capacity, retention, and support rather than by withholding core features.

Annual billing is available on Pro and Team and is discounted by two months, so an annual Pro
subscription costs eighty dollars per year rather than ninety six. Students with a verified
academic email address receive fifty percent off the Pro plan for twelve months, which brings
it to four dollars per month. The student discount may be renewed once by reverifying, giving a
maximum of twenty four discounted months. Team plans are billed per seat, and seats may be
added at any time. Adding a seat mid cycle is prorated to the day; removing a seat takes effect
at the end of the current billing period rather than immediately.

Exceeding a plan's daily notification limit does not incur an overage charge and does not drop
notifications silently. Instead, Unopsis continues to accept and store notifications but stops
sending push and email delivery for the remainder of the day, and displays a banner explaining
that the limit was reached. The counter resets at midnight in the user's configured time zone.
This behaviour was chosen deliberately over hard rejection, because losing a notification is
worse than delaying one, and over automatic billing, because a surprise charge is worse than a
delay.

Unopsis offers a fourteen day refund on any paid plan, for any reason, with no requirement to
explain. Refunds are issued to the original payment method and typically appear within five
business days. Annual subscriptions cancelled after the fourteen day window are not refunded
pro rata, but the subscription continues to the end of its paid term rather than being
terminated. There is no charge for cancelling, no retention offer process, and no requirement
to contact a human to cancel; the cancellation control is in the billing section of account
settings.
