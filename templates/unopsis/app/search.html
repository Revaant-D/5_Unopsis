ⓘ How search works

Each message, with who sent it and where, is turned into a list of numbers that captures its meaning (model {{ status.embed_model }}). Your search is turned into numbers the same way, and the closest messages are shown: up to {{ status.top_k }}, and only ones with a similarity of at least {{ status.min_score }}.

"Also answer" gives the best 3 matches to a local language model ({{ status.local_model }}), which must answer only from them and cite them as [1], [2]… The first answer after the app starts loads that model, so it can take a minute.
